ARTIST BIO

Kola Williams is a dynamic force in the Afrobeats and Hip-Hop fusion space, seamlessly blending infectious rhythms with introspective lyricism. With over 400,000 streams across major platforms and a catalog featuring four critically-acclaimed projects—including the groundbreaking TIMI mixtape and the genre-bending KARAOKE BOY EP—Kola has established himself as an artist who refuses to be boxed in. His signature red aesthetic and bold sonic choices have earned him co-signs from industry heavyweights like Mr Eazi, MI Abaga, and Teni, while collaborations with Terry Apala, Psycho YP, and Masterkraft showcase his versatility. Beyond music, Kola's multifaceted talents as a songwriter, creative director, and sound designer position him as a complete visionary shaping the future of African contemporary music. From electrifying performances at Felabration to dominating airwaves on MTV Base and Cool FM, Kola Williams is not just an emerging artist—he's a cultural movement.
